## SECTION 2: ATMOSPHERE SYNTHESIS & LIFE SUPPORT (ECLSS)
Habitat internal atmosphere is regulated by the Environmental Control and Life Support System (ECLSS). Nominal cabin atmospheric pressure is maintained at exactly 101.3 kPa, with a gas mixture of 21.2% Oxygen (O2), 78.1% Nitrogen (N2), and 0.7% Argon. The primary oxygen recycling loop utilizes catalytic Bosch CO2 reduction cells to achieve a 98.4% recovery efficiency. If the primary CO2 reduction cells experience power brownout or failure, engineering teams must immediately deploy manual backup scrubbers using Sodium Peroxide canisters (Model SP-900). If oxygen partial pressure falls below 18.5 kPa, the secondary oxygen reserve dump automatically pressurizes the living quarters.
